About MV 'Otuanga'ofa

MV 'Otuanga'ofa is the main inter-island ferry operated by the Friendly Island Shipping Agency (FISA)

Vessel Overview

Built to handle both passengers and cargo, MV 'Otuanga'ofa is equipped with modern safety and navigation systems. The vessel offers economy and VIP seating areas, sleeping cabins, onboard catering, and rest areas to ensure passenger comfort on longer voyages.

Capacity & Services

  • Passenger capacity: approximately 400
  • Vehicle deck for cars and light trucks
  • Modern safety equipment and trained crew
  • Air-conditioned VIP Lounge and economy seating
  • Snack bar and onboard facilities

Important Travel Tips

  • Please arrive at least 1 hour before departure for boarding and ticket verification.
  • Carry valid ID and booking confirmation at all times.
  • Schedules are subject to weather and operational changes.
